## Privacy Policy

You're safe here. Privacy is a right, not a feature.

I don’t want to know who you are. I don’t care what you’re doing here. This website is a quiet place — free of surveillance, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, and dark patterns.

- No cookies.
- No analytics.
- No ads.
- No social media buttons, pixels, or embedded trackers.
- No behavioral profiling.  
- No hidden scripts spying on you.

The only data collected is anonymized performance and threat detection info handled by **Cloudflare**, which sits in front of this website to protect it from abuse. I can't disable it, but it doesn’t identify you and isn’t used for marketing or profiling.

**I don't collect, store, or sell your data. Ever.**

Welcome to a small corner of the web where you're not being watched.
